* Research Interests
I work on static analysis via {{{it(abstract interpretation)}}},
formal verification and programming languages implementation.

* Education
I hold a BSc in Computer Science from Universidad Politécnica de
Madrid, and I am currently pursuing a MSc in Formal Methods in
Computer Science at both Universidad {Politécnica,Complutense} de
Madrid.

During my studies, I have specialized in the areas of declarative
programming, programming languages implementation, formal
verification, and static analysis.
