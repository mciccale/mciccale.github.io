* Research Interests
I work on static analysis via /abstract interpretation/,
formal verification, and programming languages implementation.

* Education
I hold a MSc in Formal Methods in Computer Science and Engineering at
both Universidad Politécnica and Complutense de Madrid.  Together with
a BSc in Computer Science from Universidad Politécnica de Madrid.

During my studies, I have specialized in the areas of declarative
programming, programming languages implementation, formal
verification, and static analysis.
