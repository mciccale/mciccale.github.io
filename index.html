Marco Ciccalè

I am a Ph.D. student in computer science at the IMDEA Software Institute and Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, advised by Manuel V. Hermenegildo, Jose F. Morales, and Pedro López-García. I am part of the CLIP Lab and a developer of the Ciao Prolog system.

My research sits at the intersection of static analysis, formal verification, and programming language implementation — with abstract interpretation as a central thread.

Research interests

I work on static analysis via abstract interpretation — a framework for automatically inferring program properties without running the program. My interests span the full spectrum: the theoretical foundations of abstract domains, the engineering of precise and efficient analyzers, and the integration of analysis with formal verification systems.

I am particularly interested in declarative and logic programming settings, where the clean formal semantics makes analysis both tractable and expressive.
